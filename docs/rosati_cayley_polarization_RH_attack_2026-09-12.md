# Rosati--Cayley polarization attack on RH

Date: 2026-09-12

## 1. The finite-field precedent is exactly the positivity pattern we want

For an abelian variety `A/F_q`, a polarization defines the Rosati involution `dagger` on
`End^0(A)`.  Weil's positivity theorem makes this a positive involution.  If `pi` is
Frobenius, the Weil pairing gives

    pi^dagger pi = q.

On the commutative algebra generated by `pi`, positivity forces the involution to become
ordinary complex conjugation under every complex embedding `rho`.  Therefore

    rho(pi^dagger) = overline(rho(pi))

and hence

    |rho(pi)|^2 = q.

After normalization

    U = q^(-1/2) pi,

one has

    U^dagger = U^(-1),
    |rho(U)| = 1.

For a curve this gives the Riemann hypothesis through its Jacobian.  The decisive point is
not reciprocal pairing by itself.  The decisive point is **reciprocity plus a positive
polarization which identifies the reciprocal operation with the Hilbert adjoint**.

This is the exact structural precedent for the number-field program.

References used for the collision check:

- J. S. Milne, *The Riemann Hypothesis over Finite Fields: From Weil to the Present Day*.
- J. S. Milne, *The p Riemann Hypothesis*, Theorem 1.30: `pi^dagger pi=q` and every complex
  embedding of a Frobenius eigenvalue has modulus `sqrt(q)`.

## 2. The Riemann Cayley coordinate has the identical algebraic skeleton

Use the Burnol/Nyman multiplier

    beta(s) = (s-1)/s = 1 - 1/s.

Two involutions act on it:

    shadow:       s -> 1-s       gives beta -> beta^(-1),
    real adjoint: s -> conjugate(s) gives beta -> conjugate(beta).

This is now formalized in

    GppVerify/RiemannHypothesis/CayleyShadowAdjointBridge.lean.

Away from `s=0,1`, Lean proves the exact equivalence

    beta(1-s) = conjugate(beta(s))
        <=> Re(s)=1/2.

Equivalently,

    beta(s)^(-1) = conjugate(beta(s))
        <=> |beta(s)|=1
        <=> Re(s)=1/2.

Thus the Riemann critical line is the locus where **functional-equation shadow becomes the
positive Hilbert adjoint**.

This is stronger conceptually than saying that the critical line is a convenient unitary
axis.  It identifies the missing mathematical operation: a number-field analogue of the
Rosati polarization has to make the arithmetic shadow involution into the Hilbert adjoint on
the physical spectral quotient.

## 3. Burnol's unitary operator uses exactly this Cayley multiplier

Burnol's Nyman/Lax--Phillips construction contains a unitary invariant operator `V` whose
spectral multiplier is

    (s-1)/s.

The Nyman closed subspace transforms to

    B H^2,

where `B` is the Blaschke product over zeros with `Re rho>1/2`.  His quantitative Nyman
refinement gives

    ||P_N 1||
      = product_{Re rho>1/2} |1 - 1/rho|^(m_rho)
      = |B(0)|.

Therefore the same Cayley coordinate is simultaneously

1. the normalized unitary boundary multiplier;
2. the coordinate of every bad zero in the Hardy disk;
3. the multiplicative factor in the exact Nyman projection norm.

This is not a decorative change of variables.  It is the natural arithmetic unitary chart.

## 4. Every off-line zero is an exact positive contraction defect

Write a possible right-half zero as

    rho = 1/2 + delta + i gamma,
    delta > 0.

Then

    |beta(rho)|^2
      = ((Re rho - 1)^2 + (Im rho)^2)
        / ((Re rho)^2 + (Im rho)^2)

and exactly

    1 - |beta(rho)|^2
      = 2 delta / |rho|^2
      = 2 delta / ((1/2+delta)^2 + gamma^2)
      > 0.

This real algebra is formalized in

    GppVerify/RiemannHypothesis/RiemannCayleyUnitarityBoundary.lean.

Functional-equation reflection sends

    rho -> 1-rho

and reciprocates the Cayley modulus.  Thus an off-line pair is literally

    contraction <-> expansion,

whereas a critical-line zero is exactly unitary.

The global Nyman ghost leakage is consequently

    G_ghost
      := 1 - ||P_N 1||^2
       = 1 - |B(0)|^2
       = 1 - product_{Re rho>1/2}
           (1 - 2 delta_rho/|rho|^2)^(m_rho).

Every factor lies in `(0,1)` for a bad zero.  There is no sign cancellation inside this
scalar defect: one right-half zero already makes `G_ghost>0`.

The additive version is the nonnegative ghost entropy

    E_ghost
      := -log |B(0)|^2
       = sum_{Re rho>1/2} m_rho
           [-log(1 - 2 delta_rho/|rho|^2)]
       >= 0.

It vanishes exactly when there are no right-half zeros.  At small displacement,

    -log(1-x) = x + O(x^2),

so a zero at height `gamma` and displacement `delta` contributes to first order

    2 delta/gamma^2.

This recovers, in an invariant scalar form, the `delta/gamma^2` conditioning scale already
seen in the adaptive Hardy/Cayley defect calculations.

## 5. Reflection alone still does not solve it

There are two separate no-go results which must remain explicit.

First, an internal bijection on one scalar Hardy space cannot conjugate the unilateral shift
`S` to `S*`, because

    ker S = 0,
    dim ker S* = 1.

Second, after quotienting to the ghost model space

    K_B = H^2 \ominus B H^2,

a canonical conjugation already exists and satisfies

    C_B S_B C_B = S_B*.

So even a perfect reflected forward/backward symmetry of the ghost quotient is compatible
with `K_B != 0`.

The finite skeleton is formalized in

    GppVerify/RiemannHypothesis/FiniteModelSpaceReflectionNoGo.lean.

The exact ghost obstruction is instead the rank-one contraction defect

    I - S_B S_B* = k_0^B tensor k_0^B,
    ||k_0^B||^2 = 1 - |B(0)|^2.

Reflection exchanges the incoming and outgoing defect vectors.  A Rosati-type positivity
theorem must **annihilate** the defect rather than merely pair it.

## 8. Why the finite-field precedent is useful rather than merely analogous

In the function-field proof, Poincare/Weil duality alone gives reciprocal eigenvalue pairs.
That is not enough.  The positive polarization/Rosati involution upgrades reciprocity to
unitarity.

Exactly the same logical separation has now appeared in every number-field formulation we
have tested:

    functional equation / shadow      = reciprocity,
    boundary unimodularity            = reciprocity,
    model-space canonical conjugation = reciprocity,
    Krein J-unitarity                  = reciprocity,

and none eliminates the ghost.

What RH requires is the missing second ingredient:

    positive polarization
      = OS positivity
      = de Branges positivity
      = causal positive-metric scattering
      = vanishing Nyman ghost defect.

These equivalences do not constitute a proof, but they identify the right type of theorem
with no ambiguity.  The finite-field case shows that this exact architecture is capable of
proving an RH when the positive polarization can be constructed geometrically.
