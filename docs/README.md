# GppVerify/docs

Dated working notes of the formalization: audits, syntheses, attack
notes, and plans, each carrying its date in the filename. These are the
record of how the library was built, in the order it was built. The
current status of the library is the [main README](../README.md) and the
blueprint; the notes here are history and evidence, and are superseded
where the tree has moved past them.

## Start here

- [`TATE-GUIDE.md`](TATE-GUIDE.md): the guide to the Tate layer of the
  library: every file of the local and global Tate theory, its principal
  results, and its dependencies, pinned to a commit.
- [`FORMALIZATION_PLAN.md`](FORMALIZATION_PLAN.md): the formalization
  plan and its gap analysis, including the upstream-gap register.

## The rest

The dated notes fall in loose families: the September 2026 attack and
synthesis notes (Möbius and fermionic determinant routes, orientation
and horizon syntheses, the Fredholm target), the York volume
formalization (`FS_YORK_VOLUME_FORMALIZATION_2026_10_18.md`), and audits
of specific claims (mass limits, boundary domains, coherent states).
Read them by date; a later note on the same thread supersedes the
earlier ones.
