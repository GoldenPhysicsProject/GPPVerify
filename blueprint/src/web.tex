\chapter{Overview}

\section{What this project is}

This blueprint tracks the machine-checked formalization, in Lean~4 with Mathlib, of the
\emph{shadow framework} developed by Daniel Toupin. Every statement marked
\leanok\ below has been checked by Lean's kernel. Everything else is stated
honestly as open.

\section{What it is trying to find out}

The organising question is not a single conjecture. It is a correspondence:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Analytic number theory and celestial holography appear to be describing the same
object from two sides. What is that object, and how far does the dictionary go?}
\end{quote}

On the number-theory side sit the completed zeta function $\xi(s)$, its functional
equation $s \mapsto 1-s$, the id\`ele class group $\mathbb{A}^\times/\mathbb{Q}^\times$
with its Haar measure, and the Weil explicit formula. On the holography side sit
celestial amplitudes, the principal series, Mellin transforms, and the shadow transform
$\Delta \mapsto 2-\Delta$ on conformal dimensions.

The shared structure is a \textbf{shadow involution}. Under the dictionary
$\Delta = 2s$ it is the same map on both sides, and its fixed locus is exactly the
critical line --- proved here as
\texttt{GppFE.critical\_line\_is\_fixed\_locus}: for $s \in \mathbb{C}$,
$\overline{s} = 1-s \iff \operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac12$. (The involution that matters
is $s \mapsto 1-\overline{s}$, not $s \mapsto 1-s$; the latter fixes only the single
point $s = \tfrac12$.)

Because the involution is shared, so are its consequences. That is what makes the
correspondence worth formalizing rather than merely asserting: each transfer either
survives kernel-checking or fails in a specific, nameable place.

\section{Goals, in order of ambition}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Map the dictionary precisely.} For each object on one side, identify its
    counterpart on the other, and prove the transfer or name exactly what blocks it.
  \item \textbf{Understand the shadow involution as a single mathematical object} rather
    than as two coincidentally similar symmetries.
  \item \textbf{The Riemann Hypothesis} is a major target, and RH Pathway~2
    (Spectral/Meyer) is the route developed furthest here --- but it is one goal among
    several, not the thesis of the project. The surrounding tower has value whether or
    not that pathway closes.
  \item \textbf{Physical consequences} of the same involution: CPT and time reversal,
    complete positivity, three fermion generations, the Einstein equations as a rigidity
    statement.
\end{enumerate}

\section{How to read this blueprint}

Results carry \leanok\ when the Lean proof is complete and kernel-checked. A statement
without it is open, and the surrounding text says what is missing.

Three categories are tracked separately and never conflated:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{sorry} --- \textbf{zero}, and CI fails the build if any appears.
  \item custom \texttt{axiom} --- \textbf{zero}, down from thirteen. The last one,
    \texttt{exp\_growth\_not\_tempered}, was proved on 2026-08-31 once the move to
    Mathlib 4.33 made a concrete nonzero Schwartz witness constructible.
  \item \texttt{True}-stubs --- open results deliberately parked. Every one is named
    \texttt{open\_}\ldots, and CI rejects any that is not. A file full of stubs still
    reports ``0 sorry, 0 axiom'', so the prefix is what keeps that honest.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What is deliberately not claimed.} Nobody proves RH in this repository by
accident. The value is that every reduction and constant in the surrounding tower is
kernel-checked, with each remaining gap named precisely. Where a route was tried and
failed, the failure is recorded rather than quietly dropped.

\section{Tree-to-Loop Topology (Shadow-Pair Sewing)}
\label{sec:tree-loop-sewing}

From Toupin, \emph{Loop Integrands Hidden in Trees: Explicit Extraction by Double Shadow

celestial correlator's shadow-pole analytic structure already encodes a lower-point loop
\emph{integrand}, extractable by a double shadow discontinuity. This section covers the
graph-theoretic layer of that claim, formalized in \texttt{GppTreeLoopSewing.lean} ---

\begin{theorem}[Pair-Sewing Cycle Rank]
  \label{thm:pairsewing-cyclerank}
  \lean{GppTreeLoopSewing.pairSewing_cycleRank}
  \leanok
  For a connected cubic tree with $n = 4+2L$ external leaves ($V_T = n-2$ trivalent
  vertices, $I_T = n-3$ internal edges), sewing $L$ disjoint pairs among the designated
  $2L$ extra leaves leaves $4$ external legs and $I = I_T + L = 3L+1$ internal edges, with
  cycle rank $\beta_1 = I - V_T + 1 = L$. Proved for all $L$ by direct computation on the
  vertex/edge counts (\texttt{omega}); no combinatorial machinery beyond arithmetic is
  needed since only the counts, not an explicit graph object, are formalized here.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[One-Loop Box Specialization]
  \label{cor:sixpoint-oneloop}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{What changed.} Twelve of the thirteen were not mathematical gaps at all.
Eleven declared \emph{physical observables} ($c_{2D}$, $c_{4D}^{\mathrm{Weyl}}$,

content was weakened, added, or removed --- the deduction
$c_{2D}=0 \Rightarrow n_{\mathrm{gen}}=3$ was always conditional on its physics inputs, and
now says so in its own type.

\section{Mathlib-Gap Axioms}

\textbf{There are none. As of 2026-08-31 this repository contains zero custom axioms.}

The last one, \texttt{exp\_growth\_not\_tempered} --- $e^{au}$ ($a\neq0$) is not a tempered
distribution --- is now \emph{proved}:
\lean{ExpNotTempered.exp_growth_not_tempered}\leanok\
\texttt{RiemannHypothesis/ExpNotTempered.lean}. Its only dependencies are
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, the three axioms of
Lean itself.

The argument: fix a bump $b$, smooth and supported in the unit ball, and set
\[
  \psi_n \;=\; e^{-|a|n}\, b(\,\cdot - t_n\,), \qquad t_n = \operatorname{sign}(a)\cdot n,
\]
so that $a\,t_n = |a|\,n$ whichever sign $a$ has. Two facts then collide. First
$\psi_n \to 0$ in $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C})$: shifting the bump costs only
polynomial growth in each Schwartz seminorm, $(|t_n|+1)^k$, while the scalar decays
exponentially --- so continuity of $T$ forces $T\psi_n \to T0 = 0$. Second, $T\psi_n$ does
not depend on $n$ at all: translating the bump multiplies the weighted integral by exactly
$e^{a t_n} = e^{|a|n}$, which is what the scalar was chosen to cancel, leaving
$T\psi_n = \int e^{av}b(v)\,dv$ for every $n$. A constant sequence converging to $0$ is
$0$, contradicting the positivity of that integral.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{This was not possible before the Mathlib 4.33 upgrade.}
\texttt{HasCompactSupport.toSchwartzMap} is the entry point for every construction in that
file and does not exist in Mathlib 4.19.0 --- the Schwartz space was available with no way
to build a concrete nonzero element of it, which is precisely what this argument needs. The
upgrade (2026-08-31) was undertaken partly on the expectation that it would unblock this
axiom, and it did.

\noindent\textbf{Retired here:} \texttt{hurwitz\_division\_algebra\_dimensions} (Hurwitz's
theorem: the only normed division algebras over $\mathbb{R}$ are
$\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C},\mathbb{H},\mathbb{O}$, dimensions $1,2,4,8$) was retired 2026-08-30.
It was applied by zero theorems, and the finite dimension-counting content this repo actually
uses is proved without it. Its statement survives as
\texttt{GppSM.HurwitzDimensionHypothesis}. Note that Mathlib's \texttt{NormedDivisionRing} is
associative, so the octonions do not inhabit it and the reachable content of that statement is
really the Frobenius classification $\{1,2,4\}$ --- a mismatch to fix before anyone proves or
applies it. \hfill \texttt{StandardModel/ThreeGenerations.lean}

\section{The \texttt{True}-Stub Convention (not axioms, not sorries)}

Open results are parked as \texttt{theorem foo : True := trivial} (or
\texttt{$\forall$ (\_ : True), True}) --- the repo's deliberate convention, carrying a doc
comment that names the precise upstream gap, rather than smuggling the claim in as an axiom
or leaving a live \texttt{sorry}. There are currently \textbf{151} such stubs.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{That number was wrong until 2026-08-31, and the way it was wrong is worth
recording.} This blueprint published \textbf{141}; the gate reported 142; the tree held 150 --- and
on 2026-09-01 a third pass found two more, for \textbf{152}.
The gate matched line by line --- it remembered the last \texttt{theorem} line, then looked for
\texttt{: True := trivial} on a later one --- so it could not see a stub written as
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
theorem shadow\_discontinuity :\\
\hspace*{2em}-{}- Disc(celestial amplitude) = shadow transform jump = loop integrand\\
\hspace*{2em}True := trivial
\end{quote}
because the line carrying \texttt{True := trivial} has no colon in front of \texttt{True}; the
colon is two lines up, behind a comment. Eight stubs sat in that blind spot and \emph{all eight
were unprefixed} --- \texttt{shadow\_discontinuity}, \texttt{born\_rule\_from\_haar},
\texttt{K\_compact}, \texttt{K\_trace\_class}, \texttt{plancherel\_atom\_one},
\texttt{meyer\_spectral\_weil}, \texttt{adelic\_l2\_regularization},
\texttt{digamma\_series\_form}. The gate printed ``All True-stubs correctly prefixed'' on every
build while carrying exactly the names it exists to catch. They are now renamed
\texttt{open\_}\ldots, and the gate reads whole declarations with comments stripped rather than
lines, so a stub can no longer hide behind a comment inside its own statement. The gate also now
checks this very sentence against the tree, so the published count cannot drift again.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{It took a third pass to get right.} On 2026-09-01 two further unprefixed
stubs surfaced: \texttt{thm\_universal\_shadow\_product}
(\texttt{$\forall$ (\_ : $\mathbb{C}$), True := fun \_ => trivial}) and
\texttt{scaling\_eigenspace\_ode}. Both were invisible because the gate still keyed on the
\emph{binder} being literally \texttt{True} and the \emph{proof} being literally the token
\texttt{trivial}. Neither is what makes a declaration vacuous --- \textbf{the conclusion is}.
The pattern now matches a \texttt{True} conclusion under any binders with any
\texttt{by}/\texttt{fun}/\texttt{intro} boilerplate, and is built as a \emph{union} with the
previous pattern: an intermediate "cleaner" rewrite silently dropped four stubs written as
\texttt{by intro \_} then \texttt{trivial} on the next line, so widening is now done additively
by construction. Five shapes are negative-tested.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{One stub was then closed by proof rather than by counting.}
\texttt{open\_prop\_ratio\_identity} recorded its own gap as ``follows from
\texttt{open\_thm\_shadow\_euler} by division (unconditional)'' --- that is, the only missing
ingredient was the universal shadow product, and the step from it to the ratio identity is
arithmetic. That step is now proved as
\lean{GppShadowEuler.ratio_identity_of_universal_product}\leanok, with the product supplied as
an explicit hypothesis rather than assumed globally, together with
\lean{GppShadowEuler.su3_master_of_universal_product}\leanok\ for the $SU(3)$ specialisation at
the coupling $a = 1/4$ that \texttt{shadow\_coupling\_su3} already proves correct.

This is the same move that retired twelve physics axioms in \texttt{Link6}/\texttt{DMAbundance}:
a claim parked as a vacuous \texttt{True} becomes a real theorem whose input is visible in its
own signature. The hypothesis function is deliberately arbitrary --- nothing presumes it
\emph{is} the Hadamard product, only that $\xi$ normalised at $1/2$ agrees with it.
Establishing that remains open.

\medskip
\noindent The file header of \texttt{ShadowEulerIdentity.lean} also described three of these
stubs as \textbf{axioms}. They are not. An axiom asserts its statement and downstream results
may depend on it; a \texttt{True}-stub asserts nothing and \emph{nothing can depend on it}.
Calling a stub an axiom overstates it in both directions at once. Corrected.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Every stub name now begins with \texttt{open\_}, and CI enforces it}
(\texttt{scripts/check\_stub\_naming.py}, wired into \texttt{build.yml} as a failing gate).
This closes a real hazard rather than a cosmetic one. A stub is inert and cannot corrupt a
proof, but it reports ``does not depend on any axioms'' --- the cleanest possible bill of
health --- \emph{while asserting nothing}. Until 2026-08-30 the tree therefore contained,
among others,
\begin{center}
\texttt{theorem yang\_mills\_mass\_gap : True := trivial}\\
\texttt{theorem yang\_mills\_existence : True := trivial}\\
\texttt{theorem weil\_criterion : True := trivial}\\
\texttt{theorem os\_reconstruction : True := trivial}
\end{center}
Millennium-Prize-scale problems and major open results wearing the names of the theorems
they are \emph{not}, in a repository advertising zero sorries and zero axioms. None was
referenced by any real content, so nothing unsound was ever derived from them --- but a
reader grepping the source could reasonably have concluded otherwise. The renaming makes
the hazard structurally impossible: \texttt{open\_yang\_mills\_mass\_gap} cannot be misread,
and the gate rejects any future stub that omits the prefix.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{This remains the single most important caveat for reading this repo}: a
file containing only stubs reports zero sorries and zero axioms while asserting nothing.
The full per-file, per-thread ledger (which stub covers which named gap, and the plan for
closing it) lives in \texttt{docs/FORMALIZATION\_PLAN.md}, not in this blueprint; it is
updated in the same PR that advances any thread.

\textbf{Sorry count: 0.} Enforced by a dedicated CI gate on every push
(\texttt{.github/workflows/build.yml}). Declarations named in earlier snapshots of this
chapter as carrying a live \texttt{sorry} have each since been resolved --- closed with a
real proof, converted to a named axiom above, or converted to a \texttt{True}-stub.
